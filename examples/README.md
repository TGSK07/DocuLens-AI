# Sample documents

This folder is intentionally empty in the repo — we don't ship sample invoices, CVs, or ID cards because:

  1. They're either copyrighted or contain real personal data.
  2. The app works on any document you have lying around.

To try the app, drop in one of these:

- **Invoice / receipt** — search your inbox or take a photo of a real receipt
- **Resume** — your own CV PDF
- **ID card** — a screenshot of your ID is fine for local testing (it never leaves your machine)
- **Contract** — a rental agreement, employment contract, or NDA you have on disk
- **Business card** — a photo of a card from your wallet

If you want a public sample to test with, the [RVL-CDIP](https://huggingface.co/datasets/aharley/rvl_cdip)
and [FUNSD](https://guillaumejaume.github.io/FUNSD/) datasets have thousands of
public-domain document scans.
